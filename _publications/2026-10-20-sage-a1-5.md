**Authors:** Wenjie Lou, Tianshuo Peng, Shiyang Feng, et al., including **Zelin Tan**.

Sage-A1.5 is a 35B mixture-of-experts agentic model that builds on Agents-A1 to advance long-horizon problem solving through adaptive capability composition. It combines task delegation and parallel subagent collaboration with evidence validation and reflective strategy revision, extending agentic capabilities to open scientific discovery.
