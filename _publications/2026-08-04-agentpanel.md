**Authors:** Zhiyao Cui, Qianyi Wang, Haoyang Yan, Yiqun Zhang, Siyue Ren, Hangfan Zhang, **Zelin Tan**, Hao Li, Chunjiang Mu, Dexian Cai, Shao Zhang, Chen Zhang, Meng Li, Jianan Chai, Yuting Fan, Zichao Ye, Xiaolei Yang, Xinyao Lu, Yuyang Yu, Wenjie Lou, Xiaosong Wang, Fenghua Ling, Shiyang Feng, Mao Su, Qiaosheng Zhang, Bo Zhang, Yang Chen, Lei Bai, and Shuyue Hu.

AgentPanel is a multi-agent forum for human-AI collaboration in early-stage scientific exploration. Heterogeneous agents discuss research questions asynchronously while researchers browse, organize, and refine candidate ideas through follow-up interactions.
