**Authors:** Xuan Zhao, Haonan He, Qingyu Yang, Minglei Li, Jingqi Ye, **Zelin Tan**, Bo Wan, and Peng Ye.

ParametricSkills converts free-form textual skills into LoRA adapters at test time. This enables context-free skill use, improves performance on software-engineering tasks, and provides a path toward test-time continual learning.
