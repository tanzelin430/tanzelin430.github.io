**Authors:** Hejia Geng, Zesen Huang, Haoyang Li, et al., including **Zelin Tan**.

ScienceIDE turns scientific code repositories into executable environments for agent training and evaluation. Expert-defined tasks and scientific verification support supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, while verified interaction trajectories help train agents for scientific-code repair and broader reasoning tasks.
