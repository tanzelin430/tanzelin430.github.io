**Authors:** **Zelin Tan**, Yiqun Zhang, Hao Li, Zhiyao Cui, Hejia Geng, Shao Zhang, Hangfan Zhang, Yang Chen, Xiaosong Wang, Lilong Wang, Zhenfei Yin, Shuyue Hu, Chen Zhang, and Lei Bai.

SKT is a verified synthesis pipeline for constructing skill-grounded tasks and executable trajectories from large collections of agent skills. Using 2,000 public skills, it produces 4,000 task packages and 27,164 verified trajectories, together with the held-out SkillEval benchmark.
